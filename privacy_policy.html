SixSeven

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026  ·  Effective: August 17, 2026

1. Who we are

SixSeven is a mobile application providing dating and friendship services. SixSeven is operated from Malta.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at privacy@sixsevenmeet.com.

2. What data we collect

Depending on how you use SixSeven, we may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: name, email address, date of birth or age, gender, city, country, and information used to authenticate your account.
  • Profile data: biography, profile photos, interests, preferences, and other information you choose to include in your profile.
  • Activity and interaction data: profile decisions such as Yes, No, and Maybe Later, matches, messages, and other interactions with features of the App.
  • Identity verification data: if you voluntarily choose to verify your identity, information required for the verification is processed through Stripe Identity.
  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system, push notification token, and technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

3. Why we collect and use your data

We use personal data to operate and provide SixSeven, including to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile to other users
  • Provide dating and friendship matching features
  • Process profile decisions and matches
  • Enable messaging between matched users
  • Send push notifications when enabled
  • Provide optional identity verification
  • Maintain the safety and security of the App
  • Prevent, investigate, and respond to abuse, fraud, or misuse
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of SixSeven

Where required by applicable law, we rely on an appropriate legal basis for processing personal data. This may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, compliance with legal obligations, or consent.

4. Who we share your data with

We use third-party service providers to operate SixSeven.

  • Firebase (Google): used for services including authentication, database functionality, file storage, cloud functionality, and push notifications.
  • Stripe Identity: used to provide optional identity verification.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the security of SixSeven, or in connection with legal claims.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your personal data for third-party advertising.

5. Optional identity verification

Identity verification in SixSeven is voluntary.

If you choose to verify your identity, the verification is provided through Stripe Identity. Stripe may collect and process information such as a government-issued identity document, photographs, and other information needed to complete the verification.

Users who successfully complete identity verification may receive a verification indicator or badge within the App.

SixSeven does not intentionally store copies of your government-issued identity documents in its own Firebase database. We aim to minimize the identity-verification information stored directly by SixSeven.

You can use SixSeven without completing optional identity verification unless access must be restricted in a particular situation for legal, safety, or security reasons.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the App, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account within a reasonable period, except where information must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups before being automatically removed.

7. Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have rights under applicable data protection law, including:

  • Access: request information about the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data where applicable
  • Restriction: request restriction of certain processing
  • Portability: request your data in a portable format where applicable
  • Objection: object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise your privacy rights, contact us at privacy@sixsevenmeet.com.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Malta, the supervisory authority is the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC) at idpc.org.mt.

8. International data transfers

Some of our service providers may process information outside Malta or the European Economic Area.

Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law.

9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including encrypted connections, access controls, and Firebase security mechanisms.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or personal information has been compromised, please contact us at privacy@sixsevenmeet.com.

10. Age requirement

SixSeven is intended only for adults aged 18 or older.

We do not knowingly permit people under 18 to use the App. If we become aware that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may remove the account and associated information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time when SixSeven, our service providers, or applicable legal requirements change.

When appropriate, we will notify users of material changes through the App or another suitable method.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be made available on our website.
