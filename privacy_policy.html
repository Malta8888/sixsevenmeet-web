Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026  ·  Effective: April 6, 2026

1. Who we are

SixSeven is operated by SixSeven Ltd, a company registered in Malta.

We operate the SixSeven mobile application ("the App"), which provides dating, friendship, and coaching services. You can contact us at privacy@sixsevenmeet.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: name, email address, date of birth, gender, city, country
  • Profile data: biography, profile photos, interests
  • Identity verification data: government-issued ID documents (processed by Stripe Identity)
  • Payment data: billing information, transaction history (processed by Stripe — we do not store card details)
  • Usage data: swipe activity, matches, messages, coaching session records
  • Device data: device type, operating system, push notification token

3. Why we collect your data

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under GDPR Article 6:

  • Contract performance: to provide you with the App's core services — matching, chat, and coaching
  • Legal obligation: age verification (18+), identity verification, fraud prevention
  • Legitimate interest: improving the App, preventing abuse, ensuring platform safety
  • Consent: push notifications, optional features

4. Who we share your data with

We share your data only with the following trusted third parties:

  • Firebase (Google): database, authentication, file storage, and push notifications
  • Stripe: payment processing and identity verification
  • Twilio: voice calls between users and coaches

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

5. Identity verification

To use certain features of the App, you must verify your identity and confirm you are 18 or older. This is done through Stripe Identity, which processes your government-issued ID document and a selfie. Stripe's privacy policy applies to this processing. We receive only a verified/not-verified status — we do not store copies of your ID documents.

6. Data retention

  • Active account data: retained for as long as your account is active
  • Deleted account data: removed within 30 days of account deletion
  • Payment records: retained for 7 years as required by EU law
  • Messages: deleted when both parties delete the match

7. Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Access: request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent: at any time for consent-based processing

To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@sixsevenmeet.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Malta Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC) at idpc.org.mt.

8. International transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States by our service providers (Firebase, Stripe, Twilio). These transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

9. Security

We protect your data using industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS), Firebase Security Rules, and access controls. No system is completely secure — if you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

10. Children

SixSeven is strictly for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will delete the account and all associated data immediately.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
